Dear Ann,

The garden is doing well this autumn. The persimmons are ripe, and the slip
box has grown by a hundred notes since you last came.

I finally linked the notes on bread to the ones on patience. It turns out they
were about the same thing.

Come and see it before the rains.

With love,
