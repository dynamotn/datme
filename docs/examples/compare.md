Three ways to keep a garden of notes.

## Obsidian

- price: free
- storage: local files

- ✅ Plain markdown
- ✅ Plugins for everything
- ❌ Sync costs extra

## Notion

- price: from $10
- storage: cloud

- ✅ Databases
- ❌ Slow offline
- ❌ Hard to leave

## Paper

- price: a notebook
- storage: a shelf

### Pros

- Nothing to install

### Cons

- No search
