Thin, with butter and lemon.

## Ingredients

- 200 g flour
- 2 eggs
- 300 ml milk
- A pinch of salt

## Steps

1. Whisk everything until smooth.
2. Rest for ten minutes.
3. Fry in butter, one ladle at a time.
