Recorded in the garden, on a slow afternoon.

**Ada:** Why keep notes at all, when you can search for anything?

**Grace:** Because search finds what others wrote. Notes keep what I thought.

**Ada:** And the links between them?

**Grace:** That's where new ideas come from. Two notes that never met before.
