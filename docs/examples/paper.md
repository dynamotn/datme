## Abstract

We study how a garden of notes grows when it is tended for years, and what
keeps it readable as it scales. Gardens that link more than they file stay
easier to walk.

## Introduction

Every garden is small at first. A handful of notes, a few links, one reader.

## Method

We counted the links of forty public gardens every month for two years.

## Results

The gardens with more links per note were read longer per visit.
