Asked often, answered once.

## Why a digital garden and not a blog?

A blog is a stream of finished posts. A garden is a set of notes that keep
growing, linked to each other.

## Can I reuse what is here?

Yes, with a link back. The license is at the bottom of every page.

## How often is it updated?

Whenever a note grows. The *Recent* page lists the latest ones.
