A short course on keeping notes that grow.

## Planting

Write a note about one idea, in your own words. Give it a title that says
what it claims, not what it is about.

## Linking

Link the new note to two notes you already have. Say why in a sentence next
to each link.

## Tending

Come back to a note when you learn more. Change it, split it, link it again.

## Harvesting

Write something longer from a trail of notes you already have.
